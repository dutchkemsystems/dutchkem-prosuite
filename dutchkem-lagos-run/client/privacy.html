Privacy Policy

Lagos Run — Hustle Empire · Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Data controller: Dutchkem Ventures, Lagos, Nigeria

Dutchkem Ventures ("Dutchkem", "we", "us") publishes Lagos Run — Hustle Empire (the "Game"). This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and the rights you have. It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation 2019 ("NDPR"), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") where it applies.

1. Data We Collect

  • Anonymous user ID. A random identifier stored on your device and in our database so your progress, coins and settings follow you. It is not your name, email or phone number.
  • Gameplay data. Run scores, distance, passengers carried, crashes, fleet and garage state, daily login streaks, campaign progress, mode selection and in-game purchases of virtual items.
  • Device and technical data. Browser and version, operating system, screen size, language, approximate region derived from your connection, and error diagnostics.
  • Analytics data — only if you consent. Aggregated events such as session length, screens viewed and tutorial completion, used to improve the Game.
  • Advertising data — only if you consent to personalized ads. Google AdMob may use a mobile advertising identifier and device signals to select ads. If you decline, you receive non-personalized ads.
  • Purchase receipts. When you buy virtual currency, a Season Pass or a subscription, our payment partners (Kora Pay for web and PWA, Google Play Billing for Android, Apple StoreKit for iOS) return a transaction reference, product ID, amount, currency and status. We never receive or store your full card number.

2. How We Use Data

  • To operate the Game: save and sync progress, validate runs, deliver purchased items and restore them after a reinstall.
  • To calculate leaderboards, Crew Race ghost data and daily rewards.
  • To detect cheating, fraud, chargebacks and abuse of the reward systems.
  • To fix defects and improve balance, pacing and performance.
  • To show ads that keep the Game free, and to measure whether they were shown.
  • To answer support requests and to comply with legal obligations, including tax and consumer law.

3. Legal Basis

  • Contract — processing needed to provide the Game, your account ID, your purchases and your saved progress.
  • Consent — analytics and personalized advertising. You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings without losing access to the Game.
  • Legitimate interests — security, anti-cheat, fraud prevention and service reliability, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — retaining transaction records where tax or consumer law requires it.

4. Sharing

We share the minimum necessary with processors bound by written data-processing terms:

  • Kora Pay — payment processing for web and PWA purchases.
  • Google AdMob — advertising and ad measurement.
  • Google Play — Android billing and app distribution.
  • Apple — iOS in-app purchases and App Store distribution.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics.
  • Hosting and database providers — servers and storage that run the Game.

We do not sell your personal data. Some processors are outside Nigeria; transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the processor's standard contractual clauses.

5. Retention

Anonymous gameplay records are kept while your installation is active and for up to 24 months afterwards for leaderboards and anti-cheat. Analytics events are kept for up to 14 months. Purchase and tax records are kept for 6 years as required by Nigerian financial law. Error diagnostics are kept for 90 days.

6. Your Rights

  • Access a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Export your gameplay data in a portable JSON file (Settings → Export My Data).
  • Delete your data (Settings → Delete My Data), subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics or personalized ads at any time.
  • Object to, or request restriction of, processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or with your EU supervisory authority if you are in the EU.

Requests are answered within 30 days. Write to privacy@dutchkem.com.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, do not serve personalized ads to them and do not permit purchases by them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic uses TLS 1.3. Sensitive values are signed and verified with the Web Crypto API on the client and with server-side HMAC verification on the backend. Server credentials are held in environment secrets, never in the client bundle. Access to production data is limited and logged.
